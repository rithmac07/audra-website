Audra was built by one person, but not alone. Here's what it stands on.

Program

Audra was created for the Kode With Klossy Mobile App Challenge, a program that gave the app its first real deadline and its first real audience.

Frameworks and technology

Audra is built with SwiftUI for its interface, AVFoundation and AVAudioEngine for audio playback and processing, the Accelerate framework for real-time FFT analysis, and Core Haptics for beat-synced feedback. Music search and previews are powered by Apple's iTunes Search API.

Design assistance

Claude AI was used throughout development as a thinking partner, helping with architecture decisions, async and await migration, and tuning the emotion fingerprint algorithm.

Inspiration

Audra exists because most music technology is designed for people who can hear it. This app is a small attempt to change that, built with the deaf and hard-of-hearing community in mind at every step.

Thank you

To everyone who tested an early build, gave feedback, or just asked how it was going: thank you. This app is better because of you.
