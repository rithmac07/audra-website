2. What Audra does

Audra is a music accessibility app designed for deaf and hard-of-hearing listeners. It analyzes audio in real time to generate visual and haptic representations of music, including a frequency visualizer, a rhythm grid, and an emotion fingerprint. Song previews and metadata are sourced from the iTunes Search API.
