Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Your privacy matters, especially in an app built around personal hearing profiles. This policy explains what Audra does and doesn't do with your information.

1. What Audra stores on your device

Audra keeps your personalized hearing profile, onboarding preferences, and appearance settings (like dark or light mode) stored locally on your device. This information is not uploaded to a server or shared with third parties.

2. What Audra does not collect

Audra does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, or contact information, and does not track your listening history for advertising purposes. There are no third-party analytics or ad trackers built into the app.

3. Third-party services

Audra uses the iTunes Search API to find songs and retrieve 30-second previews. When you search for music, your query is sent to Apple's servers to return results. Please refer to Apple's own privacy policy for details on how they handle that data.

4. Microphone and audio access

If Audra requests access to audio playback or device audio, that access is used solely to power the app's real-time visualizer, rhythm grid, and emotion fingerprint features. Audio data is processed in the moment and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

5. Data deletion

Since your data lives only on your device, deleting the app removes your hearing profile and preferences along with it. There's no server-side account to separately delete.

6. Children's privacy

Audra does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app doesn't collect personal data in the first place, there's no account information to worry about at any age.

7. Changes to this policy

If Audra's data practices change, for example if a future version adds cloud sync, this page will be updated to reflect that before the change takes effect.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact links on the Audra website.
